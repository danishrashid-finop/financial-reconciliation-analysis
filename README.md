Financial Reconciliation & Risk Analysis (SQL)

Objective
To reconcile system recorded payments with bank settlements and identify financial risks such as missing payments, unauthorized credits, amount mismatches, and settlement delays.

Tools Used

 1. SQL (SQLite)
 2. Excel (data creation & preprocessing)

Key Analysis Performed

. Identified system payments not reflected in bank statements
. Detected bank credits without corresponding system records
. Flagged amount mismatches between system and bank
. Calculated settlement delays using date arithmetic
. Ranked departments by unreconciled financial exposure

Business Impact

. Highlights high-risk departments and transactions
. Enables audit prioritization
. Improves financial control and transparency
. Key Skills Demonstrated
. SQL joins (LEFT JOIN, INNER JOIN)
. Data cleaning & validation
. Date manipulation and delay analysis
. Risk quantification and prioritization
. Analytical thinking and business interpretation
